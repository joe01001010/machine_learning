# IMDb sentiment reference

## Overall verdict
<!-- recommend recommendation rating rated stars overall verdict watch -->
Look for the reviewer's own recommendation, rating, or final assessment. In a mixed review, a clear verdict usually carries more weight than an isolated compliment or criticism. Keep the reviewer's opinion separate from any opinions they quote.

## Narrative and screenplay
<!-- plot story screenplay script writing dialogue ending characters twist premise -->
Distinguish plot summary from judgment. A reviewer can enjoy a film about unpleasant events. Look for evaluations of coherence, originality, dialogue, and character development. Check whether praise of the premise is followed by criticism of its execution.

## Direction and vision
<!-- director directing direction tone worldbuilding vision scene scenes transitions atmosphere -->
Look for opinions about how the director handles tone, atmosphere, and transitions. An unsettling or bleak tone may be praised when it serves the film. A shift described as confusing or unintentionally funny is more likely a criticism.

## Acting and performances
<!-- acting actor actors actress cast casting chemistry performance performances ensemble lead -->
Distinguish the quality of the performance from whether the character is likable. Praise for one actor can coexist with an unfavorable verdict on the film. Give more weight to the reviewer's overall assessment of the cast and the film.

## Visuals and cinematography
<!-- cinematography camera shot shots visuals effects lighting framing costume costumes design animation -->
Look for evaluations of the framing, lighting, effects, and production design. A dark image or restrained palette can be deliberate. Praise such as beautiful photography can coexist with a negative judgment on the film as a whole.

## Sound and music
<!-- music score soundtrack sound audio mixing songs song composer silence -->
Look for opinions about how the score and sound support the film, whether dialogue can be heard, and whether the mix distracts. Judge the reviewer's reaction in context. A tense or dissonant score can be a compliment.

## Pacing and editing
<!-- pacing pace editing rhythm slow drag rushed runtime length cuts continuity boring tedious -->
Separate a deliberately slow pace from complaints that a film drags or loses momentum. A short runtime can be praised for efficiency or criticized for rushing. Weigh pacing judgments alongside the reviewer's final verdict.

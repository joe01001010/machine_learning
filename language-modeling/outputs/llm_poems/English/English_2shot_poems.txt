=== Poem 1 ===
Dawn breaks soft, mist hangs low.  
Rain taps slow on roof and road.  
World still sleeps, quiet, deep.  
Wet leaves glisten, cool and steep.  

Clouds part just a little more.  
Drip by drip, the sky grows clear.  
Morning breaths in silver light.  
Ground drinks in, no sound, no fight.

=== Poem 2 ===
The river flows, slow and low,  
Through fields of green and old stone.  
It whispers to the willow,  
No sound but peace and tone.  

Sunlight dapples on the water,  
Reflects the sky’s soft blue.  
No rush, no need, no hurry—  
Just stillness, deep and true.

=== Poem 4 ===
Stars blink above the silent road,  
Wind hums low, no word to speak.  
Footsteps fade where shadows grow,  
Night holds breath, and I am free.  

Moon spills silver on the trail,  
Distant hills in hush are still.  
No map, no name, just stars to guide—  
I walk with sky, and time, and will.

=== Poem 5 ===
The frost slips away, soft and slow.  
Birds return with a chirp and a call.  
Earth stirs beneath a warm, golden sky.  
Flowers push through the soil, bold and tall.  

Rain falls in gentle, silver streams.  
Green fingers reach for the sun’s light.  
Wind hums old songs through the trees.  
Spring wakes up, and the world takes flight.

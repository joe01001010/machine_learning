=== Poem 1 ===

=== Poem 2 ===
The river flows soft through green fields.  
It murmurs low, no sound to hear.  
Sunlight glides on water's skin.  
Still, it moves with gentle will.  

Leaves drift by, unbothered, slow.  
Wind hums near, a quiet song.  
No footstep breaks its peace.  
Time stands still where it runs.

=== Poem 3 ===
Sunset painted the kitchen wall.  
Dad hummed while he stirred the pot.  
Mama’s hands folded bread with care.  
Warmth filled the room, soft and deep.  

Rain tapped on the windowpane.  
We sat by the fire, quiet, close.  
Old stories wrapped in candlelight.  
Home felt like a lullaby.

=== Poem 4 ===
Stars blink above the silent plain.  
Wind hums low, no sound to name.  
Footsteps fade where shadows creep.  
Night holds breath, then stills the deep.  

Moon glides near, a silver eye.  
Distant light, a fading sigh.  
We walk on dreams, no map to guide.  
Stars speak in hush, not in pride.

=== Poem 5 ===
The snow melts from the field's cold edge.  
Buds push through the earth, soft and new.  
Birds return with songs of return.  
Sunlight warms the waking land.

Daffodils bloom in yellow streams.  
Wind stirs the trees with green delight.  
Frogs croak in ponds, low and clear.  
Life hums in every leaf and stone.

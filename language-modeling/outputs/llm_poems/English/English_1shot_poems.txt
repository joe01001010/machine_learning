=== Poem 1 ===
Dawn breaks soft, mist curls low.  
Rain taps leaves, quiet and slow.  
World wakes in silvered hush.  
Damp air breathes, cool and lush.  

Pavements gleam, wet and bright.  
Clouds drift near, soft and light.  
No sound but drops on stone.  
Morning stirs, gentle, alone.

=== Poem 2 ===
The river flows soft through the meadow.  
It whispers to willows, calm and low.  
No rush, no sound, just quiet time.  
It moves like breath in the early light.  

It bends around stones, slow and deep.  
Reflects the sky in stillness.  
No one speaks, no one near.  
Just water, wind, and the silence.

=== Poem 4 ===
Stars blink above the silent plain,  
Wind hums a tune we both have known.  
Footsteps fade where shadows lean,  
Night holds us close, no need to go.  

Moon spills silver on the trail,  
Dust dances in the quiet air.  
We walk with no destination,  
Just stars and silence, and the prayer.

=== Poem 5 ===
The snow melts from the garden paths.  
Birds return with songs of praise.  
Buds push through the cold earth's hold.  
Green light fills the morning haze.  

Daffodils dance in the breeze.  
Flowers open in the sun.  
The wind carries a fresh, sweet scent.  
Spring wakes the sleeping land.
